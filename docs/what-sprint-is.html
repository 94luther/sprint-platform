Part 1 · What just happened to delivery apps

Africa's app graveyard

The big consumer delivery apps spent millions and still lost money on nearly every order.

Jumia Foodclosed in 7 countries, Dec 2023
Bolt Foodleft Nigeria and South Africa
Glovoleft Ghana after 3 years
GoLemonLagos groceries, gone 2026

The reason is simple. Winning one app customer costs around P900 in marketing, and they need about 15 orders before you break even. In a mid-size city, most people never get there, because nobody wants to download another app for bread.
