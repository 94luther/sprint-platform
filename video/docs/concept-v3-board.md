# Sprint concept film V3 board: the 30 second merchant-first cut
Built 2026-08-29 from council rulings G1-G6 (see concept-v2-board.md). 900
frames, 30.0s, 1920x1080, 30fps. Owner goal binding: a viewer must understand
what the product does, see it on a customer's phone, on another company's
website, in WhatsApp, and feel the value proposition. Business owners first.

Beat 1 THE WEBSITE, 0 to 6s: frame one IS Thato's Bakery's website with the one
Sprint green button "Order. Sprint delivers." Cursor clicks about 2s in, Sprint
checkout slides over the shop's own page with Orange Money, MyZaka, Smega,
Cash. Type: "Give your shop a delivery button." Caption: "The shop keeps its
customer. Sprint does the delivery." (No ink title open, per council G1.)

Beat 2 THE TILL, 6 to 12s: order card lands on the shop device: "2 loaves and
milk / Block 8 . P57" with Accept. Tap Accept about 8.3s, status flips to
"Rider on the way", payout chip lands IN THE SAME BEAT: "P42.75 to you on
delivery." Type: "Orders hit your till. Money follows." (Council G3: till not
phone, payout with the accept.)

Beat 3 WHATSAPP, 12 to 17s: customer texts "2 loaves and milk to Block 8",
order card with P57 and payment chips, Orange Money tap, "Paid. Track your
rider:" with link chip. Type: "Customers can order straight in WhatsApp."

Beat 4 THE RIDER, 17 to 23s: real Veo rider/arrival footage FULL FRAME and
bright (council G6: the physical machine must be seen), small corner tracking
card with live map and "Arrives by 14:35 or we call you". Type: "A real rider,
watched the whole way."

Beat 5 THE MONEY, 23 to 25s, two seconds only (council G4): P90 splits fast
into P67.50 to the merchant, P16.20 to the rider, P6.30 to Sprint. Type:
"Everyone paid, automatically."

Beat 6 CLOSE, 25 to 30s: cheetah mark, skewed SPRINT lockup, orange slash,
"Built for Gaborone. Moving with you.", small "Gaborone, delivered." No node
network animation (council G1: PowerPoint fluff).

Dropped from v2.1: the consumer app catalog beat (council G1: contradicts the
infrastructure pitch in a merchant-first cut; the customer's phone is still
shown via WhatsApp and tracking). The 47s v2.1 cut keeps the app beat and
remains the fuller leadership version. Both exist; the owner decides.
