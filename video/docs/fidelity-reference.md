# Sprint fidelity reference sheet
The Fidelity seat checks every asset in any Sprint film or graphic against this
sheet before it ships. Anything that does not match is blocked. Created
2026-08-30 after a generic non-uniform stock rider shipped in the V4 explainer.

## What is CONFIRMED and owned
- Logo: use public\logo-mark.png ONLY. Never trace it, never a lookalike, never
  rebuild the cheetah in code. (Standing rule, already in memory.)
- Wordmark: SPRINT in the skewed lockup with the orange slash, from brand.ts.
- Colours: green #3AAA35, orange #F7941D, ink #111111, bone #F4F4F1 (brand.ts).
- Money: always pula, always labelled with whose it is.
- Place: Gaborone. Real app screens from public\appdemo, never invented UI,
  never stretched.

## What is a GAP, needed from Luther before any rider is shown as real
The V4 rider clip is AI-generated Veo STOCK: a generic man in a plain black
jacket by a plain black motorbike. It is NOT a Sprint rider. It has no uniform,
no branded top box, no Sprint colours. The Fidelity seat now blocks this.
To show a real Sprint rider we need from Luther, any of:
- Photos or short clips of actual Sprint riders in uniform on Sprint bikes.
- The uniform spec (colour, logo placement) and the bike or top-box branding.
Until those exist, a rider on screen must be EITHER an explicitly Sprint-branded
illustration (green and orange, cheetah mark on the box) OR carry a small honest
on-screen note that the footage is a placeholder. Never a generic stock human
presented as a Sprint driver.

## The check, run on every asset
1. Is the rider in Sprint uniform on a Sprint-branded bike? If not, block or
   label as placeholder.
2. Are the screens real app captures, unstretched?
3. Is the logo the real mark, money in labelled pula, place clearly Gaborone?
A film does not pass until every shot clears all three.
